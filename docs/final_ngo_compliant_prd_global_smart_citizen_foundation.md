# NGO-Compliant Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## Global Smart Citizen Foundation Platform

---

# 1. Document Information

## Project Name
Global Smart Citizen Foundation Platform

## Organization
Global Smart Citizen Foundation

## Document Type
NGO-Compliant Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Version
Final Version 3.0

## Purpose of this Document
This document defines the functional, operational, technical, administrative, security, and compliance requirements for the Global Smart Citizen Foundation digital platform.

The platform is intentionally designed as:
- A citizen awareness ecosystem
- A volunteer coordination platform
- A social participation network
- A community engagement system
- A public support and assistance platform

The platform is strictly positioned as an NGO/community initiative and intentionally avoids:
- MLM/network marketing structures
- Commission-based systems
- Referral income models
- Recruitment-based earnings
- Pyramid-style financial structures

The system shall operate as:
> A social awareness, volunteer engagement, and citizen participation platform.

# 5. User Roles & Organizational Structure

## 5.1 Guest Visitor
A visitor who has not registered on the platform.

### Permissions
- View public website
- Read awareness content
- Learn about foundation mission
- Access public information
- Register as Smart Citizen

---

## 5.2 Smart Citizen
A registered member of the foundation.

### Purpose
A Smart Citizen is an aware and socially connected citizen participating in foundation initiatives and awareness activities.

### Permissions
- Login to dashboard
- Access Smart Citizen ID
- Participate in awareness campaigns
- Share awareness participation links
- Invite citizens to awareness initiatives
- Donate voluntarily
- Access Need Help functionality
- View personal participation statistics
- Update personal profile

---

## 5.3 Active Smart Citizen
An internally identified Smart Citizen who actively participates in awareness campaigns and foundation activities.

### Eligibility Indicators
- Voluntary contribution/support participation
- Awareness participation
- Community engagement activity
- Consistent platform participation

### Important Note
This status is for internal engagement tracking only and does not represent financial entitlement.

---

## 5.4 Volunteer
A Smart Citizen who actively contributes to social awareness and foundation activities.

### Volunteer Eligibility
A Smart Citizen may apply to become a Volunteer after:
- Helping expand awareness participation within the community
- Demonstrating active participation in awareness initiatives for a minimum period defined by the foundation
- Consistently supporting foundation campaigns and outreach activities

### Important Note
Volunteer status is:
- Participation-based
- Activity-based
- Approval-based
- Organizationally assigned

Volunteer status is NOT:
- A financial entitlement
- A commission level
- A referral income tier
- An earning guarantee

---

## 5.5 Coordinator Roles

The foundation may assign operational coordination responsibilities based on:
- Long-term participation
- Community outreach contribution
- Volunteer engagement
- Awareness campaign involvement
- Citizen participation impact
- Organizational support
- Regional coordination involvement

Role assignments remain fully under administrative approval and organizational review.

These are organizational/community management responsibilities only.

---

### Coordinator
Supports volunteer engagement and awareness activities.

### Block Coordinator
Supports block-level community coordination and awareness initiatives.

### District Coordinator
Supports district-level volunteer management and awareness campaigns.

### State Coordinator
Supports state-level coordination and regional awareness activities.

### National Coordinator
Supports national-level awareness initiatives and strategic coordination.

---

## 5.6 Admin
The administrative authority of the platform.

### Admin Rights
Admins will have complete platform control and operational transparency.

### Admin Permissions
Admins can:
- Manage all users
- Manage all volunteers
- Manage all coordinators
- Access complete analytics
- Access all records
- Manage donations/contributions
- Perform CRUD operations on all profiles
- Manage awareness campaigns
- Manage events
- Approve/reject volunteers
- Assign roles and permissions
- Moderate profiles and content
- Configure platform settings
- Manage notifications
- View participation analytics
- View geographic analytics
- Manage abuse reports
- Suspend/delete accounts

# 7. Need Help Functionality

## Module Purpose
The Need Help module is a community support and professional assistance system where approved volunteers may make their profiles publicly discoverable.

---

## Visibility Rules
Only volunteers who:
- Have completed volunteer verification
- Have explicitly consented to public profile visibility
- Have enabled public profile access

shall appear in the Need Help section.

---

## Supported Profiles
Examples include:
- Teachers
- Lawyers
- Doctors
- Educators
- Consultants
- Social workers
- Community volunteers

---

## User Access
Only registered Smart Citizens can access the Need Help section.

---

## Contact Options
Users may contact volunteers through:
- Platform contact request
- WhatsApp redirect
- Public phone number (optional and consent-based)

---

## Privacy Controls
Volunteers must have:
- “Show phone publicly” toggle
- Public profile visibility toggle
- Profile visibility management
- Request profile removal option
